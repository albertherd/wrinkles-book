About Paul Caruana

Paul Caruana is a celebrated Maltese artist known for his ability to paint a life that has now disappeared. Growing up in the capital city of Valletta, his watercolors document the social history of the Maltese Islands.

His work serves as both a comprehensive art collection and a memoir of those formative years. Structuring personal recollections as "visual fairytales," his softly executed watercolors recount this Mediterranean ethnography with humour, warmth, and deep nostalgia.
